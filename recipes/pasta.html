Pasta

Description

A simple spaghetti with a quick tomato sauce.

It takes about 25 minutes and needs only a few ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 250 g spaghetti
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 400 g canned crushed tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • Black pepper and basil to taste

Steps

  1. Boil a large pot of salted water.
  2. Cook the spaghetti for about 10 minutes, then drain.
  3. Add the tomatoes, salt and pepper, and simmer for10 minutes.
  4. Toss the pasta in the sauce and serve with basil.
