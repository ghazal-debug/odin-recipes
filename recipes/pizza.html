Homemade pizza

Description

A delicious homemade pizza with tomato sauce and cheese.

Ingredients

  • Pizzza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Prepara the pizza dough
  2. Spread tomato sauce over the dough.
  3. Add mozzarella cheese.
  4. Bake until the crust is golden and the cheese melts.
