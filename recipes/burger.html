Homemade Burger

Description

A delicious Homemade burger with a juicy beef patty, fresh lettuce, tomato, and cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 burger bun
  • 150 g ground beef
  • 1 slice of cheese
  • 1 lettuce leaf
  • 2 tomato slices
  • 1 tablespoon ketchup

Steps

  1. Shape the ground beff into a patty.
  2. Cook the patty in a pan until fully cooked.
  3. Place the cheese on the patty.
  4. put the patty unside the bun.
  5. Add lettuce, tomato, and ketchup
  6. serve and enjoy!
